Projects

Research Projects

  1. Expanding Market Opportunities for Maine's Marine Aquaculture Industry [Spring 2024 – Spring 2025]

    NOAA-funded study of market pathways, supply chain dynamics, and consumer preferences in Maine's marine aquaculture sector, examining barriers to market penetration for farmed seafood across distribution and consumer awareness. Graduate research assistant to Dr. Keith S. Evans and Dr. Caroline L. Noblet, University of Maine, contributing data analysis, literature review, and stakeholder communication. Manuscript in preparation.

  2. Assessing Maine Hunters' Behavior in the Time of PFAS [Fall 2024 – Summer 2026]

    Survey-based study of how PFAS contamination in wildlife affects the behavior of licensed Maine hunters, measuring current knowledge and concern about contaminants and testing the effectiveness of messaging in hunting advisories. Facilitated by the Maine Department of Inland Fisheries and Wildlife. Graduate research assistant to Dr. Caroline Noblet and Dr. Dianne Kopec.

  3. Assessing Maine's Anglers' Preferences for Fish Consumption [Fall 2024 – Summer 2025]

    Study of fish consumption habits and consumption rates among Maine freshwater anglers, facilitated by the Maine Department of Inland Fisheries and Wildlife. Findings inform the Maine Center for Disease Control's assessment of the need for and scope of fish consumption advisories.

  4. Pathways for Bangladesh's Transition to Clean Energy [2023 – 2024]

    Study of sustainable pathways for Bangladesh's energy transformation toward green and clean energy, conducted at SANEM in collaboration with the Tara Climate Foundation, Singapore. Research assistant contributing data analysis and report writing.

  5. Multi-disciplinary Study on Bangladesh's Economic Security [April 2024 – June 2024]

    Research assistant on a study facilitated by the Department of Foreign Affairs and Trade, Australian High Commission, under national consultants Dr. Selim Raihan and Dr. Lailufar Yasmin.

  6. Assessment of Other Government Agencies and Infrastructure for Clearance of Perishable Cargo at the Border Crossing Points of Akhaura, Tamabil, and Sonamasjid [April 2023 – May 2024]

    Research assistant on an Asian Development Bank–funded assessment of trade facilitation infrastructure at three Bangladesh–India border crossings, under international consultant Dr. Selim Raihan.

  7. Internal Migration and Economic Growth in Bangladesh [February 2022 – March 2022]

    Study funded by the International Labour Organization, supervised by Dr. Mahbubul Hakim, Associate Professor, Department of Economics, Shahjalal University of Science and Technology.

  8. Impact of Foreign Direct Investment on Economic Growth: A Panel Data Approach among South Asian Countries, 2002–2021 [November 2022 – December 2022]

    Undergraduate thesis, supervised by Associate Professor Gias Uddin Khan, Department of Economics, Shahjalal University of Science and Technology.
